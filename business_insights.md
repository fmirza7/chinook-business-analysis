# Business Insights — Chinook Store Analysis

## 1. Revenue is concentrated in a few markets

* **Finding:** USA is the top revenue market.
* **Supporting number:** USA: $523.06 vs. $303.96 Canada; $2,328.60 total across 24 countries.
* **Action:** Prioritize USA/Canada retention while testing campaigns in mid-tier markets.

## 2. Rock dominates genre sales

* **Finding:** Rock is the best-selling genre.
* **Supporting number:** Rock: $826.65 vs. $382.14 Latin and $261.36 Metal.
* **Action:** Expand Rock offerings and cross-promote Metal and Alternative & Punk.

## 3. Top customers drive a disproportionate share of revenue

* **Finding:** The top 10 customers generate a significant share of revenue.
* **Supporting number:** 10 of 59 customers (~17%) generated $451.20 (~19.4%) of total revenue.
* **Action:** Introduce a loyalty/VIP program for top spenders.

## 4. Revenue is flat year-over-year

* **Finding:** Revenue shows no clear growth trend.
* **Supporting number:** Annual revenue remained between $449–$481 from 2021–2025.
* **Action:** Pursue growth through catalog expansion, new artists, or bundle pricing.

## 5. Iron Maiden is the top-performing artist

* **Finding:** Iron Maiden generates the most artist revenue.
* **Supporting number:** Iron Maiden: $138.60 vs. $105.93 for U2 and $90.09 for Metallica.
* **Action:** Feature top artists in targeted Rock/Metal promotions.

## 6. Most customers are medium-value

* **Finding:** The customer base is concentrated in the Medium-Value tier.
* **Supporting number:** 54 of 59 customers are Medium-Value, generating $2,093.50.
* **Action:** Target Medium-Value customers with upselling campaigns.

## 7. Repeat purchases drive high customer value

* **Finding:** Top spenders show similar purchase frequency, suggesting repeat buying drives revenue.
* **Supporting number:** The top 5 spenders each placed 7 orders; average order value is $5.65.
* **Action:** Focus retention efforts on maintaining purchase frequency through new-release notifications and similar campaigns.
